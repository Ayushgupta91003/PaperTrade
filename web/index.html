How to read candles

The body spans open to close. Wicks mark the high and low. Green means the close was at or above the open; red means it was below. A doji has a very small body. A hammer shape has a long lower wick. Engulfing means one candle's body covers the previous opposite-color body. These labels describe price shapes.
